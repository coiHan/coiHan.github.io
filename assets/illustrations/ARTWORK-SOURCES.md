# Selected artwork

All four selected assets were created with the built-in imagegen tool and saved as transparent WebP. They are generated illustrations, not documentary astronomical photography.

- `solar-atlas-handdrawn.webp`: nine-cell colored-pencil/gouache illustration atlas, from imagegen `exec-556ecb42-319c-4b58-8b1b-5e14b888db94`. Final instruction: completely redraw the photorealistic draft as visibly human-hand-painted flat editorial gouache and colored pencil, broad opaque strokes, irregular silhouettes, restrained cream/ochre/terracotta/sage/ink palette; 3×3 order Sun/Mercury/Venus, Earth/Mars/Jupiter, Saturn/Uranus/Neptune; transparent gaps; no realistic surfaces, typography or watermark. The Sun and Earth cells are overridden by the separately selected assets below.
- `earth-asia-handdrawn.webp`: separate Earth from imagegen `exec-7036d383-02b8-45fc-9910-5ff3a2d5b024`, matching the atlas. Final instruction: center the globe around 110°E, 25°N so China and East Asia face the viewer; show simplified East Asian coast, Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia, India, Siberia and a glimpse of Australia; sage land, ink-blue ocean and loose off-white cloud strokes; do not obscure China; visible gouache/pencil marks; no political borders, labels or photographic texture.
- `sun-handdrawn-soft.webp`: original hand-drawn golden disk with short rounded rays, no face. Complete prompt and reference record: [sun-handdrawn-source.txt](sun-handdrawn-source.txt).
- `thinking-sheep.webp`: temporary thinking cartoon avatar requested by the owner. Complete prompt: [avatar-source.txt](avatar-source.txt). PNG was converted to WebP at quality 86 for the website; transparency retained.

The earlier realistic atlas and smooth cartoon sun were rejected drafts and are not deployed. Source PNGs are retained locally for editing. Atlas windows are selected by CSS; artwork pixels were not redrawn by code.
